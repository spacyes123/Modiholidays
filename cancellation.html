Proposed Cancellation & Refund Schedule

Proposed Version 2.1 · Draft only · No effective date

OWNER & LEGAL REVIEW DRAFT — NOT APPROVED, ACTIVE OR EFFECTIVE. This page is a proposal for review, not a current cancellation policy or promise of a refund. Proposed Version 2.1 remains inactive. No unapproved percentage, deduction, deadline or process should be applied to a customer.

This proposed schedule is intended to accompany the proposed company-wide Modi Holidays Standard T&C for domestic, international, family, group and customised tours. It would apply only after owner approval, Indian legal review and a separately authorised activation. The applicable customer quotation and booking confirmation must identify the approved version and any properly disclosed supplier-specific exception.

1. Cancellation request and effective date

The proposal says a cancellation request must be sent in writing through an authorised Modi Holidays email address or WhatsApp number. It would take effect when received by the authorised channel, regardless of when staff acknowledge or process it. The authorised channels must be confirmed and communicated before this proposal can be approved.

2. Date calculation

The proposed calculation uses Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+05:30) and calendar dates. The cancellation receipt date and first scheduled travel-service departure date would be converted to IST. The departure date is day zero; the difference between the dates determines the proposed band.

3. Proposed standard cancellation schedule

The following percentages are proposals from owner-review Version 2.1 only. They are not current customer charges.

Calendar days before departureProposed standard charge
61 days or more10%
60–30 days25%
29–15 days60%
14–8 days75%
7 days or fewer, including no-shows100%

Under the proposal, a full-booking cancellation charge would be calculated against the total package value, subject to applicable law and properly disclosed supplier-specific exceptions. The schedule must not be used unless approved and included in the applicable customer documents.

4. Supplier-specific charges and no duplicate recovery

A separately identified non-refundable supplier commitment could be charged only if it was disclosed and accepted before booking, permitted by law and supported by actual documented costs. Overlapping charges, supplier recoveries and relevant credits would be accounted for to prevent double recovery. The final deduction would have an itemised calculation.

5. Partial cancellations

For one traveller cancelling, the proposed standard deduction applies to that traveller’s attributable package value, rather than automatically to the entire group booking. Supplier charges must meet the same disclosure and no-duplicate-recovery requirements. Any increase in shared costs for remaining travellers would be explained and accepted in writing before an additional charge is imposed.

6. Refund calculation and communication

The proposal calls for a written refund calculation within five business days after receipt of a valid written cancellation request. If supplier information is outstanding, a provisional calculation would identify unresolved amounts. This proposed process must not delay a refund legally due earlier. The calculation would show payments received, permitted deductions, supplier recoveries and credits, and the net refund.

7. Proposed refund processing

The proposal states that an approved refund would be processed within 14 calendar days after the cancellation and refund amount are confirmed, subject to applicable law and valid, disclosed supplier-specific conditions. Any legally required earlier deadline would take precedence. Refunds would ordinarily use the original payment method, subject to applicable payment-processing requirements. These are proposed targets, not an active service commitment.

8. Supplier changes or cancellations

If a supplier changes or cancels a service, available alternatives, deductions and refund eligibility would depend on the accepted booking terms, properly disclosed supplier conditions and applicable law. Modi Holidays would communicate available options. Nothing in this draft removes statutory consumer rights or guarantees a particular supplier remedy.

9. Contact

For a current travel enquiry, contact modiholidays15@gmail.com or +91 76660 18401. The official channels for serving a cancellation request remain to be confirmed by the owner and must be supplied in any approved booking documents.

Owner and legal review required

Confirm the percentage schedule and no-show treatment; IST and day-counting; what constitutes receipt on each authorised channel; supplier-cost evidence, credits and partial-cancellation allocation; the five-business-day calculation notice; when a refund amount is considered confirmed; the proposed 14-day processing period; and any required consumer-law disclosures. No fixed refund amount, tax rate, bank account, payment charge or historical quotation percentage has been imported.

See the Proposed Standard Terms & Conditions for the draft document-precedence, supplier-exception and existing-booking protections. Both pages remain inactive owner/legal review drafts.

Do not apply this draft to an existing booking or customer request. Existing bookings retain the terms accepted for that booking; a later version does not apply retroactively.
