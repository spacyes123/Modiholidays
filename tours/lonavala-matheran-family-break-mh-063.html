Lonavala & Matheran Family Break

Customisable Maharashtra holiday featuring Mumbai, Lonavala, Matheran.

Places covered

Mumbai → Lonavala → Matheran

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrive in Mumbai; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Gateway of India, Marine Drive and heritage precincts. Overnight in Mumbai.

Day 2

Travel from Mumbai to Lonavala; visit Bhushi Dam area and Tiger Point. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Lonavala.

Day 3

Travel from Lonavala to Matheran; visit Charlotte Lake and Panorama Point. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Matheran.

Day 4

After breakfast, check out and depart from Matheran. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
