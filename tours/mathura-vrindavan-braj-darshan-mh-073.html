Mathura Vrindavan Braj Darshan

Customisable Uttar Pradesh holiday featuring Mathura, Vrindavan, Govardhan.

Places covered

Mathura → Vrindavan → Govardhan → Mathura

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Mathura; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi and Vishram Ghat. Overnight in Mathura.

Day 2

Travel from Mathura to Vrindavan; visit Banke Bihari Temple and Prem Mandir. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Vrindavan.

Day 3

Travel from Vrindavan to Govardhan; visit Govardhan Hill and Kusum Sarovar. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Govardhan.

Day 4

Travel from Govardhan to Mathura; visit Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi and Vishram Ghat. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Mathura.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Mathura. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
