Ashtavinayak Temple Circuit

Customisable Maharashtra holiday featuring Pune, Morgaon, Siddhatek.

Places covered

Pune → Morgaon → Siddhatek → Pali → Mahad → Theur → Lenyadri → Ozar → Ranjangaon

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Pune; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Shaniwar Wada and Aga Khan Palace. Overnight in Pune.

Day 2

Travel from Pune to Morgaon; visit Mayureshwar Ganpati Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Morgaon.

Day 3

Travel from Morgaon to Siddhatek; visit Siddhivinayak Ganpati Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Siddhatek.

Day 4

Travel from Siddhatek to Pali; visit Ballaleshwar Ganpati Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Pali.

Day 5

Travel from Pali to Mahad; visit Varadvinayak Ganpati Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Mahad.

Day 6

Travel from Mahad to Theur; visit Chintamani Ganpati Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Theur.

Day 7

Travel from Theur to Lenyadri; visit Girijatmaj cave temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Lenyadri.

Day 8

Travel from Lenyadri to Ozar; visit Vighneshwar Ganpati Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Ozar.

Day 9

Travel from Ozar to Ranjangaon; visit Mahaganapati Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Ranjangaon.

Day 10

After breakfast, check out and depart from Ranjangaon. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
