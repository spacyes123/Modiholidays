Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Dubai; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Burj Khalifa area, Dubai Mall and Al Fahidi district. Overnight in Dubai.

Day 2

Travel from Dubai to Abu Dhabi; visit Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and Louvre Abu Dhabi. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Abu Dhabi.

Day 3

Travel from Abu Dhabi to Dubai; visit Burj Khalifa area, Dubai Mall and Al Fahidi district. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Dubai.

Day 4

After breakfast, check out and depart from Dubai. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
