Ayodhya Lucknow Heritage

Customisable Uttar Pradesh holiday featuring Lucknow, Ayodhya, Lucknow.

Places covered

Lucknow → Ayodhya → Lucknow

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrive in Lucknow; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Bara Imambara and Rumi Darwaza. Overnight in Lucknow.

Day 2

Travel from Lucknow to Ayodhya; visit Ram Mandir area and Sarayu riverfront. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Ayodhya.

Day 3

Travel from Ayodhya to Lucknow; visit Bara Imambara and Rumi Darwaza. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Lucknow.

Day 4

After breakfast, check out and depart from Lucknow. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
