Spiti Valley Mountain Journey

Customisable holiday itinerary — request a confirmed quotation

Places covered

Narkanda → Sangla → Tabo → Kaza → Kalpa → Shimla

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in Narkanda. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Hatu Peak approach and apple orchard scenery. Overnight in Narkanda.

Day 2

Visit Hatu Peak approach and apple orchard scenery. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Narkanda.

Day 3

Visit Hatu Peak approach and apple orchard scenery; allow time for local photography and independent exploration. Continue sightseeing in Narkanda at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Narkanda.

Day 4

Travel from Narkanda to Sangla (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Baspa River valley and Kamru Fort surroundings. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Sangla.

Day 5

Travel from Sangla to Tabo (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Tabo Monastery and Spiti valley landscape. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Tabo.

Day 6

Travel from Tabo to Kaza (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Key Monastery and Kibber village (road and altitude permitting). Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Kaza.

Day 7

Visit Langza village and high-altitude viewpoints (permits/weather permitting). Continue sightseeing in Kaza at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Kaza.

Day 8

Travel from Kaza to Kalpa (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Kinnaur Kailash viewpoints and Kalpa village. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Kalpa.

Day 9

Travel from Kalpa to Shimla (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit The Ridge, Christ Church and Mall Road. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Shimla.

Day 10

After breakfast, check out from Shimla and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
