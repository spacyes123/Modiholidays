North Goa → South Goa

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in North Goa. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Fort Aguada, Candolim and Calangute beaches. Overnight in North Goa.

Day 2

Visit Fort Aguada, Candolim and Calangute beaches. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in North Goa.

Day 3

Visit Anjuna beach area and local markets. Continue sightseeing in North Goa at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in North Goa.

Day 4

Travel from North Goa to South Goa (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Colva and Benaulim beaches. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in South Goa.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out from South Goa and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
