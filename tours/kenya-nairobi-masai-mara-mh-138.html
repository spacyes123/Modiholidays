Kenya Nairobi Masai Mara

Customisable Kenya holiday featuring Nairobi, Lake Naivasha, Maasai Mara.

Places covered

Nairobi → Lake Naivasha → Maasai Mara → Nairobi

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Nairobi; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Giraffe Centre and Nairobi National Museum. Overnight in Nairobi.

Day 2

Travel from Nairobi to Lake Naivasha; visit lake scenery and optional boat trip. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Lake Naivasha.

Day 3

Travel from Lake Naivasha to Maasai Mara; visit wildlife reserve and optional authorised game drives. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Maasai Mara.

Day 4

Travel from Maasai Mara to Nairobi; visit Giraffe Centre and Nairobi National Museum. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Nairobi.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Nairobi. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
