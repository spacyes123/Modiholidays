Canada Rockies & Vancouver

Customisable Canada holiday featuring Vancouver, Whistler, Banff.

Places covered

Vancouver → Whistler → Banff → Calgary

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Vancouver; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Stanley Park and waterfront. Overnight in Vancouver.

Day 2

Travel from Vancouver to Whistler; visit alpine village and mountain viewpoints. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Whistler.

Day 3

Travel from Whistler to Banff; visit Banff National Park and Lake Louise. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Banff.

Day 4

Travel from Banff to Calgary; visit Calgary Tower area and city heritage. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Calgary.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Calgary. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
