Paris Swiss Highlights

Customisable France & Switzerland holiday featuring Paris, Lucerne, Interlaken.

Places covered

Paris → Lucerne → Interlaken → Zurich

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Paris; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Eiffel Tower area and Seine riverfront. Overnight in Paris.

Day 2

Travel from Paris to Lucerne; visit Chapel Bridge and lakefront. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Lucerne.

Day 3

Travel from Lucerne to Interlaken; visit lakeside scenery and optional mountain excursion. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Interlaken.

Day 4

Travel from Interlaken to Zurich; visit Old Town and Lake Zurich. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Zurich.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Zurich. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
