Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Rome; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Colosseum area and Roman Forum. Overnight in Rome.

Day 2

Travel from Rome to Florence; visit Duomo and Ponte Vecchio. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Florence.

Day 3

Travel from Florence to Venice; visit St Mark’s Square and Grand Canal. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Venice.

Day 4

Travel from Venice to Milan; visit Duomo and Galleria Vittorio Emanuele. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Milan.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Milan. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
