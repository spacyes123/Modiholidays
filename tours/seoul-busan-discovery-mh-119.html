Seoul Busan Discovery

Customisable South Korea holiday featuring Seoul, Gyeongju, Busan.

Places covered

Seoul → Gyeongju → Busan

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Seoul; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Gyeongbokgung Palace and Bukchon Hanok Village. Overnight in Seoul.

Day 2

Travel from Seoul to Gyeongju; visit Bulguksa Temple and heritage sites. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Gyeongju.

Day 3

Travel from Gyeongju to Busan; visit Haeundae Beach and Gamcheon Culture Village. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Busan.

Day 4

After breakfast, check out and depart from Busan. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
