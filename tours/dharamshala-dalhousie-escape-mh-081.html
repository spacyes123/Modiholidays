Dharamshala Dalhousie Escape

Customisable Himachal Pradesh holiday featuring Dharamshala, McLeod Ganj, Dalhousie.

Places covered

Dharamshala → McLeod Ganj → Dalhousie → Khajjiar

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrive in Dharamshala; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Dalai Lama Temple complex and tea gardens. Overnight in Dharamshala.

Day 2

Travel from Dharamshala to McLeod Ganj; visit Bhagsu Waterfall and monastery precinct. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in McLeod Ganj.

Day 3

Travel from McLeod Ganj to Dalhousie; visit Gandhi Chowk and hill viewpoints. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Dalhousie.

Day 4

Travel from Dalhousie to Khajjiar; visit Khajjiar meadow and cedar forest. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Khajjiar.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Khajjiar. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
