Istanbul Cappadocia Pamukkale

Customisable Turkey holiday featuring Istanbul, Cappadocia, Pamukkale.

Places covered

Istanbul → Cappadocia → Pamukkale → Istanbul

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Istanbul; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Hagia Sophia area and Bosphorus waterfront. Overnight in Istanbul.

Day 2

Travel from Istanbul to Cappadocia; visit Göreme Open Air Museum and optional balloon flight. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Cappadocia.

Day 3

Travel from Cappadocia to Pamukkale; visit travertine terraces and Hierapolis. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Pamukkale.

Day 4

Travel from Pamukkale to Istanbul; visit Hagia Sophia area and Bosphorus waterfront. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Istanbul.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Istanbul. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
