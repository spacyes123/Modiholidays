Mumbai Heritage & Elephanta

Customisable Maharashtra holiday featuring Mumbai, Elephanta Caves, Mumbai.

Places covered

Mumbai → Elephanta Caves → Mumbai

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrive in Mumbai; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Gateway of India, Marine Drive and heritage precincts. Overnight in Mumbai.

Day 2

Travel from Mumbai to Elephanta Caves; visit Elephanta rock-cut cave temples. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Elephanta Caves.

Day 3

Travel from Elephanta Caves to Mumbai; visit Gateway of India, Marine Drive and heritage precincts. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Mumbai.

Day 4

After breakfast, check out and depart from Mumbai. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
