Places covered

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in Jaipur. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Amber Fort, Jal Mahal viewpoint and Hawa Mahal façade. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 2

Visit Amber Fort, Jal Mahal viewpoint and Hawa Mahal façade. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 3

Visit City Palace, Jantar Mantar and old Jaipur bazaars. Continue sightseeing in Jaipur at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 4

Travel from Jaipur to Jodhpur (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Jodhpur.

Day 5

Visit Clock Tower market and blue-city viewpoints. Continue sightseeing in Jodhpur at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Jodhpur.

Day 6

Travel from Jodhpur to Jaisalmer (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli and Gadisar Lake. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Jaisalmer.

Day 7

After breakfast, check out from Jaisalmer and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
