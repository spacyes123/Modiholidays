Domestic · Kerala · 6 Days / 5 Nights

Kerala Kanyakumari Explorer

Customisable Kerala holiday featuring Kochi, Munnar, Alleppey.

Places covered

Kochi → Munnar → Alleppey → Kovalam → Kanyakumari

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrive in Kochi; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets and St Francis Church. Overnight in Kochi.

Day 2

Travel from Kochi to Munnar; visit Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point and tea estates. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Munnar.

Day 3

Travel from Munnar to Alleppey; visit backwater canals and optional houseboat cruise. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Alleppey.

Day 4

Travel from Alleppey to Kovalam; visit Lighthouse Beach and coastal viewpoints. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Kovalam.

Day 5

Travel from Kovalam to Kanyakumari; visit Vivekananda Rock Memorial ferry and seafront. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Kanyakumari.

Day 6

After breakfast, check out and depart from Kanyakumari. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
