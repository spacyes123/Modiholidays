Saurashtra Pilgrimage

Customisable Gujarat holiday featuring Ahmedabad, Dwarka, Porbandar.

Places covered

Ahmedabad → Dwarka → Porbandar → Somnath → Gir

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Ahmedabad; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Sabarmati Ashram and Adalaj Stepwell. Overnight in Ahmedabad.

Day 2

Travel from Ahmedabad to Dwarka; visit Dwarkadhish Temple and Gomti Ghat. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Dwarka.

Day 3

Travel from Dwarka to Porbandar; visit Kirti Mandir and coastal promenade. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Porbandar.

Day 4

Travel from Porbandar to Somnath; visit Somnath Temple and seafront. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Somnath.

Day 5

Travel from Somnath to Gir; visit Gir landscape and optional permitted safari. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Gir.

Day 6

After breakfast, check out and depart from Gir. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
