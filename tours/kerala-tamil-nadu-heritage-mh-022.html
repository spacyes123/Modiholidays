Domestic · Kerala · 14 Days / 13 Nights

Kerala Tamil Nadu Heritage

Customisable holiday itinerary — request a confirmed quotation

Places covered

Kochi → Munnar → Thekkady → Kanyakumari → Rameswaram → Madurai

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrival in Kochi. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets and St Francis Church. Overnight in Kochi.

Day 2

Visit Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets and St Francis Church. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Kochi.

Day 3

Visit Mattancherry Palace area, Jew Town and spice market. Continue sightseeing in Kochi at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Kochi.

Day 4

Travel from Kochi to Munnar (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point and Kundala Lake. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Munnar.

Day 5

Visit Tea Museum (opening days permitting), tea gardens and Pothamedu Viewpoint. Continue sightseeing in Munnar at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Munnar.

Day 6

Travel from Munnar to Thekkady (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Periyar Lake boating (tickets permitting) and spice plantation visit. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Thekkady.

Day 7

Visit Kumily spice market and optional guided nature walk (authorised operators only). Continue sightseeing in Thekkady at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Thekkady.

Day 8

Travel from Thekkady to Kanyakumari (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Vivekananda Rock Memorial ferry (sea conditions permitting) and Thiruvalluvar Statue viewpoint. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Kanyakumari.

Day 9

Visit Kanyakumari seafront and sunset point. Continue sightseeing in Kanyakumari at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Kanyakumari.

Day 10

Travel from Kanyakumari to Rameswaram (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Ramanathaswamy Temple and Agni Theertham. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Rameswaram.

Day 11

Visit Pamban Bridge viewpoint and Dhanushkodi road (access permitting). Continue sightseeing in Rameswaram at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Rameswaram.

Day 12

Travel from Rameswaram to Madurai (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Meenakshi Amman Temple and Puthu Mandapam area. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Madurai.

Day 13

Visit Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace and local flower market. Continue sightseeing in Madurai at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Madurai.

Day 14

After breakfast, check out from Madurai and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
