Manali and Kufri Holiday

Customisable holiday itinerary — request a confirmed quotation

Places covered

Manali → Kufri → Shimla

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrival in Manali. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Hadimba Devi Temple, Old Manali and Manu Temple area. Overnight in Manali.

Day 2

Visit Hadimba Devi Temple, Old Manali and Manu Temple area. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Manali.

Day 3

Visit Vashisht hot springs area and Beas River viewpoints. Continue sightseeing in Manali at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Manali.

Day 4

Travel from Manali to Kufri (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Kufri hill viewpoints and Himalayan Nature Park (subject to opening). Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Kufri.

Day 5

Visit Kufri hill viewpoints and Himalayan Nature Park (subject to opening); allow time for local photography and independent exploration. Continue sightseeing in Kufri at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Kufri.

Day 6

Travel from Kufri to Shimla (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit The Ridge, Christ Church and Mall Road. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Shimla.

Day 7

Visit Jakhoo Temple and nearby heritage viewpoints. Continue sightseeing in Shimla at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Shimla.

Day 8

After breakfast, check out from Shimla and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
