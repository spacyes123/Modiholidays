Kashmir Winter Snow Escape

Customisable Kashmir holiday featuring Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam.

Places covered

Srinagar → Gulmarg → Pahalgam → Srinagar

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Srinagar; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Dal Lake, Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh. Overnight in Srinagar.

Day 2

Travel from Srinagar to Gulmarg; visit Gulmarg meadow and optional Gondola. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Gulmarg.

Day 3

Travel from Gulmarg to Pahalgam; visit Lidder River and optional Aru/Betaab valleys. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Pahalgam.

Day 4

Travel from Pahalgam to Srinagar; visit Dal Lake, Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Srinagar.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Srinagar. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
