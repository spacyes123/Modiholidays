Kaziranga Shillong Getaway

Customisable holiday itinerary — request a confirmed quotation

Places covered

Guwahati → Kaziranga → Shillong

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrival in Guwahati. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Kamakhya Temple and Brahmaputra riverfront. Overnight in Guwahati.

Day 2

Visit Kamakhya Temple and Brahmaputra riverfront. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Guwahati.

Day 3

Visit Umananda Temple ferry excursion (river conditions permitting). Continue sightseeing in Guwahati at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Guwahati.

Day 4

Travel from Guwahati to Kaziranga (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Kaziranga National Park authorised jeep safari (seasonal/permit dependent). Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Kaziranga.

Day 5

Visit Kaziranga orchid and biodiversity park area. Continue sightseeing in Kaziranga at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Kaziranga.

Day 6

Travel from Kaziranga to Shillong (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Ward’s Lake, Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians and Police Bazaar. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Shillong.

Day 7

After breakfast, check out from Shillong and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
