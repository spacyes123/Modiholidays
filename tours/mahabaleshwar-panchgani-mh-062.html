Mahabaleshwar & Panchgani

Customisable Maharashtra holiday featuring Pune, Panchgani, Mahabaleshwar.

Places covered

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Pune; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Shaniwar Wada and Aga Khan Palace. Overnight in Pune.

Day 2

Travel from Pune to Panchgani; visit Table Land and Sydney Point. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Panchgani.

Day 3

Travel from Panchgani to Mahabaleshwar; visit Venna Lake and Arthur Seat. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Mahabaleshwar.

Day 4

After breakfast, check out and depart from Mahabaleshwar. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
