Domestic · Tamil Nadu · 6 Days / 5 Nights

Tamil Nadu Temple Heritage

Customisable Tamil Nadu holiday featuring Chennai, Kanchipuram, Thanjavur.

Places covered

Chennai → Kanchipuram → Thanjavur → Madurai → Rameswaram

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrive in Chennai; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Marina Beach and Kapaleeshwarar Temple. Overnight in Chennai.

Day 2

Travel from Chennai to Kanchipuram; visit Ekambareswarar Temple and silk-weaving precinct. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Kanchipuram.

Day 3

Travel from Kanchipuram to Thanjavur; visit Brihadeeswarar Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Thanjavur.

Day 4

Travel from Thanjavur to Madurai; visit Meenakshi Amman Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Madurai.

Day 5

Travel from Madurai to Rameswaram; visit Ramanathaswamy Temple and Pamban Bridge viewpoints. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Rameswaram.

Day 6

After breakfast, check out and depart from Rameswaram. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
