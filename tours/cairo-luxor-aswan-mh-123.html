Places covered

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Cairo; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Pyramids of Giza and Egyptian Museum (venue to confirm). Overnight in Cairo.

Day 2

Travel from Cairo to Luxor; visit Karnak Temple and Valley of the Kings. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Luxor.

Day 3

Travel from Luxor to Aswan; visit Philae Temple and Nile riverfront. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Aswan.

Day 4

Travel from Aswan to Cairo; visit Pyramids of Giza and Egyptian Museum (venue to confirm). Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Cairo.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Cairo. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
