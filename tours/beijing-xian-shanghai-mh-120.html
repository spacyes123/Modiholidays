Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Beijing; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Forbidden City and Great Wall excursion. Overnight in Beijing.

Day 2

Travel from Beijing to Xi’an; visit Terracotta Army museum. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Xi’an.

Day 3

Travel from Xi’an to Shanghai; visit The Bund and Yu Garden. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Shanghai.

Day 4

After breakfast, check out and depart from Shanghai. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
