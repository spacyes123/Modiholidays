Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Amman; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Amman Citadel and Roman Theatre. Overnight in Amman.

Day 2

Travel from Amman to Petra; visit Petra Treasury and Siq. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Petra.

Day 3

Travel from Petra to Dead Sea; visit Dead Sea shoreline and resort access. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Dead Sea.

Day 4

Travel from Dead Sea to Amman; visit Amman Citadel and Roman Theatre. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Amman.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Amman. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
