Domestic · Kashmir · 7 Days / 6 Nights

Kashmir Family Discovery

Lakes, valleys and mountain scenery

Places covered

Srinagar → Gulmarg → Pahalgam

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrival in Srinagar. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Dal Lake shikara viewpoint and Boulevard Road; optional shikara ride. Overnight in Srinagar.

Day 2

Visit Dal Lake shikara viewpoint and Boulevard Road; optional shikara ride. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Srinagar.

Day 3

Visit Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh and Chashme Shahi Mughal gardens. Continue sightseeing in Srinagar at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Srinagar.

Day 4

Travel from Srinagar to Gulmarg (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Gulmarg meadow and the St Mary’s Church area. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Gulmarg.

Day 5

Visit Gulmarg Gondola (subject to tickets and weather), Kongdori viewpoints. Continue sightseeing in Gulmarg at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Gulmarg.

Day 6

Travel from Gulmarg to Pahalgam (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Lidder River promenade and Pahalgam market. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Pahalgam.

Day 7

After breakfast, check out from Pahalgam and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
