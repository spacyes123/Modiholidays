Ho Chi Minh Mekong Phu Quoc

Customisable Vietnam holiday featuring Ho Chi Minh City, Mekong Delta, Phu Quoc.

Places covered

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Ho Chi Minh City; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see War Remnants Museum and central heritage. Overnight in Ho Chi Minh City.

Day 2

Travel from Ho Chi Minh City to Mekong Delta; visit river waterways and village landscapes. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Mekong Delta.

Day 3

Travel from Mekong Delta to Phu Quoc; visit island beaches and optional cable car. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Phu Quoc.

Day 4

After breakfast, check out and depart from Phu Quoc. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
