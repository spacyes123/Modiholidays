Places covered

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in Indore. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Rajwada Palace area and Sarafa Bazaar. Overnight in Indore.

Day 2

Visit Rajwada Palace area and Sarafa Bazaar. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Indore.

Day 3

Visit Lal Bagh Palace and local heritage precinct. Continue sightseeing in Indore at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Indore.

Day 4

Travel from Indore to Ujjain (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Mahakaleshwar Temple and Mahakal Lok corridor. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Ujjain.

Day 5

Visit Harsiddhi Temple and Ram Ghat. Continue sightseeing in Ujjain at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Ujjain.

Day 6

Travel from Ujjain to Mandu (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Jahaz Mahal, Hindola Mahal and Rani Roopmati Pavilion. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Mandu.

Day 7

Visit Jahaz Mahal, Hindola Mahal and Rani Roopmati Pavilion; allow time for local photography and independent exploration. Continue sightseeing in Mandu at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Mandu.

Day 8

Travel from Mandu to Bhopal (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Upper Lake waterfront and Tribal Museum. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Bhopal.

Day 9

After breakfast, check out from Bhopal and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
