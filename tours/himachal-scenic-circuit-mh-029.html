Himachal Scenic Circuit

Customisable holiday itinerary — request a confirmed quotation

Places covered

Amritsar → Dalhousie → Khajjiar → Dharamshala → Manali

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in Amritsar. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Golden Temple and heritage streets. Overnight in Amritsar.

Day 2

Visit Golden Temple and heritage streets. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Amritsar.

Day 3

Visit Jallianwala Bagh and Attari–Wagah ceremony (schedule permitting). Continue sightseeing in Amritsar at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Amritsar.

Day 4

Travel from Amritsar to Dalhousie (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Gandhi Chowk, Subhash Baoli and St John’s Church. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Dalhousie.

Day 5

Visit Panchpula and nearby hill viewpoints. Continue sightseeing in Dalhousie at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Dalhousie.

Day 6

Travel from Dalhousie to Khajjiar (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Khajjiar meadow, lake area and cedar forest viewpoints. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Khajjiar.

Day 7

Visit Khajjiar meadow, lake area and cedar forest viewpoints; allow time for local photography and independent exploration. Continue sightseeing in Khajjiar at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Khajjiar.

Day 8

Travel from Khajjiar to Dharamshala (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit McLeod Ganj, Tsuglagkhang temple complex and Tibetan market. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Dharamshala.

Day 9

Visit Bhagsu Nag Temple, Bhagsu waterfall approach and Dal Lake area. Continue sightseeing in Dharamshala at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Dharamshala.

Day 10

Travel from Dharamshala to Manali (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Hadimba Devi Temple, Old Manali and Manu Temple area. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Manali.

Day 11

After breakfast, check out from Manali and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
