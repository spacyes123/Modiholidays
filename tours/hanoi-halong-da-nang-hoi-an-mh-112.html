Hanoi Halong Da Nang Hoi An

Customisable Vietnam holiday featuring Hanoi, Ha Long Bay, Da Nang.

Places covered

Hanoi → Ha Long Bay → Da Nang → Hoi An

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrive in Hanoi; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Old Quarter and Hoan Kiem Lake. Overnight in Hanoi.

Day 2

Travel from Hanoi to Ha Long Bay; visit Ha Long Bay cruise scenery (operator-dependent). Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Ha Long Bay.

Day 3

Travel from Ha Long Bay to Da Nang; visit Marble Mountains and My Khe Beach. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Da Nang.

Day 4

Travel from Da Nang to Hoi An; visit Hoi An Ancient Town and lantern streets. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Hoi An.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Hoi An. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
