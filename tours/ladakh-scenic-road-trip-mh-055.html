Domestic · Ladakh · 10 Days / 9 Nights

Ladakh Scenic Road Trip

Customisable holiday itinerary — request a confirmed quotation

Places covered

Leh → Sham Valley → Nubra Valley → Pangong Lake

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrival in Leh. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Shanti Stupa and Leh market; acclimatise and avoid exertion. Overnight in Leh.

Day 2

Visit Shanti Stupa and Leh market; acclimatise and avoid exertion. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Leh.

Day 3

Visit Leh Palace, Hall of Fame museum and local monastery. Continue sightseeing in Leh at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Leh.

Day 4

Travel from Leh to Sham Valley (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Magnetic Hill viewpoint, Sangam of Indus and Zanskar rivers and Alchi Monastery. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Sham Valley.

Day 5

Visit Magnetic Hill viewpoint, Sangam of Indus and Zanskar rivers and Alchi Monastery; allow time for local photography and independent exploration. Continue sightseeing in Sham Valley at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Sham Valley.

Day 6

Travel from Sham Valley to Nubra Valley (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Diskit Monastery and Maitreya Buddha statue. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Nubra Valley.

Day 7

Visit Hunder sand dunes and optional double-humped camel ride. Continue sightseeing in Nubra Valley at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Nubra Valley.

Day 8

Travel from Nubra Valley to Pangong Lake (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Pangong Tso lakeshore viewpoints; high-altitude precautions. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Pangong Lake.

Day 9

Visit Scenic lakeside sunrise and return via permitted mountain route. Continue sightseeing in Pangong Lake at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Pangong Lake.

Day 10

After breakfast, check out from Pangong Lake and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
