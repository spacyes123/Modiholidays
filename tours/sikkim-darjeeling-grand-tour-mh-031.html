Sikkim Darjeeling Grand Tour

Places covered

Darjeeling → Pelling → Gangtok → Lachung → Ravangla

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in Darjeeling. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Tiger Hill sunrise (weather permitting) and Ghoom Monastery. Overnight in Darjeeling.

Day 2

Visit Tiger Hill sunrise (weather permitting) and Ghoom Monastery. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Darjeeling.

Day 3

Visit Batasia Loop, Himalayan Mountaineering Institute and tea garden viewpoint. Continue sightseeing in Darjeeling at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Darjeeling.

Day 4

Travel from Darjeeling to Pelling (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Pemayangtse Monastery and Rabdentse ruins. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Pelling.

Day 5

Visit Skywalk area and Khecheopalri Lake (road permitting). Continue sightseeing in Pelling at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Pelling.

Day 6

Travel from Pelling to Gangtok (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit MG Marg, Enchey Monastery and Tashi Viewpoint. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Gangtok.

Day 7

Visit Rumtek Monastery and Banjhakri Falls (road permitting). Continue sightseeing in Gangtok at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Gangtok.

Day 8

Travel from Gangtok to Lachung (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Lachung village and Yumthang Valley (permits/season permitting). Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Lachung.

Day 9

Visit Lachung village and Yumthang Valley (permits/season permitting); allow time for local photography and independent exploration. Continue sightseeing in Lachung at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Lachung.

Day 10

Travel from Lachung to Ravangla (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Buddha Park and Temi Tea Garden viewpoint. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Ravangla.

Day 11

Visit Buddha Park and Temi Tea Garden viewpoint; allow time for local photography and independent exploration. Continue sightseeing in Ravangla at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Ravangla.

Day 12

After breakfast, check out from Ravangla and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
