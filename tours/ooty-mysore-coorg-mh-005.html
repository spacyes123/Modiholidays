Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in Ooty. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Government Botanical Garden, Ooty Lake and Rose Garden. Overnight in Ooty.

Day 2

Visit Government Botanical Garden, Ooty Lake and Rose Garden. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Ooty.

Day 3

Visit Doddabetta Peak and tea factory viewpoint (subject to opening). Continue sightseeing in Ooty at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Ooty.

Day 4

Travel from Ooty to Mysore (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Mysore Palace and Devaraja Market. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Mysore.

Day 5

Visit Chamundi Hill and Brindavan Gardens (opening permitting). Continue sightseeing in Mysore at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Mysore.

Day 6

Travel from Mysore to Coorg (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Abbey Falls and Raja’s Seat. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Coorg.

Day 7

Visit Coffee plantation visit and Dubare area (activity availability permitting). Continue sightseeing in Coorg at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Coorg.

Day 8

After breakfast, check out from Coorg and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
