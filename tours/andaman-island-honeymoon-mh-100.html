Andaman Island Honeymoon

Customisable Andaman holiday featuring Port Blair, Havelock, Neil Island.

Places covered

Port Blair → Havelock → Neil Island → Port Blair

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Port Blair; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Cellular Jail and Corbyn’s Cove. Overnight in Port Blair.

Day 2

Travel from Port Blair to Havelock; visit Radhanagar Beach. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Havelock.

Day 3

Travel from Havelock to Neil Island; visit Bharatpur and Laxmanpur beaches. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Neil Island.

Day 4

Travel from Neil Island to Port Blair; visit Cellular Jail and Corbyn’s Cove. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Port Blair.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Port Blair. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
