Shimla Manali Honeymoon

Customisable Himachal Pradesh holiday featuring Shimla, Kullu, Manali.

Places covered

Shimla → Kullu → Manali → Shimla

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Shimla; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see The Ridge, Mall Road and Christ Church. Overnight in Shimla.

Day 2

Travel from Shimla to Kullu; visit Kullu valley viewpoints. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Kullu.

Day 3

Travel from Kullu to Manali; visit Hadimba Temple and Solang Valley. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Manali.

Day 4

Travel from Manali to Shimla; visit The Ridge, Mall Road and Christ Church. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Shimla.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Shimla. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
