Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Barcelona; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Sagrada Família and Gothic Quarter. Overnight in Barcelona.

Day 2

Travel from Barcelona to Madrid; visit Royal Palace and Prado Museum precinct. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Madrid.

Day 3

Travel from Madrid to Seville; visit Real Alcázar and Plaza de España. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Seville.

Day 4

After breakfast, check out and depart from Seville. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
