Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in Delhi. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Qutub Minar and Humayun’s Tomb. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 2

Visit Qutub Minar and Humayun’s Tomb. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 3

Visit India Gate, Rajpath area and Old Delhi heritage lanes. Continue sightseeing in Delhi at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 4

Travel from Delhi to Agra (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Taj Mahal (closed Fridays) and Agra Fort. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Agra.

Day 5

Travel from Agra to Jaipur (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Amber Fort, Jal Mahal viewpoint and Hawa Mahal façade. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 6

After breakfast, check out from Jaipur and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
