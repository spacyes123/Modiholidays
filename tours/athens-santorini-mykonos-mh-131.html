Athens Santorini Mykonos

Customisable Greece holiday featuring Athens, Santorini, Mykonos.

Places covered

Athens → Santorini → Mykonos → Athens

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrive in Athens; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Acropolis and Plaka. Overnight in Athens.

Day 2

Travel from Athens to Santorini; visit Oia viewpoints and caldera. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Santorini.

Day 3

Travel from Santorini to Mykonos; visit Little Venice and windmills. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Mykonos.

Day 4

Travel from Mykonos to Athens; visit Acropolis and Plaka. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Athens.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Athens. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
