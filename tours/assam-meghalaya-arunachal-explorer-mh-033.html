Assam Meghalaya Arunachal Explorer

Customisable holiday itinerary — request a confirmed quotation

Places covered

Guwahati → Dirang → Tawang → Kaziranga → Shillong

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in Guwahati. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Kamakhya Temple and Brahmaputra riverfront. Overnight in Guwahati.

Day 2

Visit Kamakhya Temple and Brahmaputra riverfront. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Guwahati.

Day 3

Visit Umananda Temple ferry excursion (river conditions permitting). Continue sightseeing in Guwahati at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Guwahati.

Day 4

Travel from Guwahati to Dirang (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Dirang Dzong and Sangti Valley. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Dirang.

Day 5

Visit Hot-water spring area and mountain valley scenery. Continue sightseeing in Dirang at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Dirang.

Day 6

Travel from Dirang to Tawang (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Tawang Monastery and War Memorial. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Tawang.

Day 7

Visit Sela Pass viewpoints and optional Bum La route (special permit required). Continue sightseeing in Tawang at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Tawang.

Day 8

Travel from Tawang to Kaziranga (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Kaziranga National Park authorised jeep safari (seasonal/permit dependent). Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Kaziranga.

Day 9

Visit Kaziranga orchid and biodiversity park area. Continue sightseeing in Kaziranga at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Kaziranga.

Day 10

Travel from Kaziranga to Shillong (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Ward’s Lake, Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians and Police Bazaar. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Shillong.

Day 11

Visit Elephant Falls and Shillong Peak (opening/permissions permitting). Continue sightseeing in Shillong at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Shillong.

Day 12

After breakfast, check out from Shillong and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
