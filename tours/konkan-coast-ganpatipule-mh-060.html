Konkan Coast & Ganpatipule

Customisable Maharashtra holiday featuring Mumbai, Alibaug, Ganpatipule.

Places covered

Mumbai → Alibaug → Ganpatipule → Ratnagiri

Your day-by-day journey

Suggested itinerary. Attractions, travel times and arrangements are subject to your written quote and local conditions.

Day 1

Arrive in Mumbai; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Gateway of India, Marine Drive and heritage precincts. Overnight in Mumbai.

Day 2

Travel from Mumbai to Alibaug; visit Alibaug Beach and Kolaba Fort viewpoints. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Alibaug.

Day 3

Travel from Alibaug to Ganpatipule; visit Ganpatipule Temple and beach. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Ganpatipule.

Day 4

Travel from Ganpatipule to Ratnagiri; visit Ratnadurg Fort and Thibaw Palace. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Ratnagiri.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Ratnagiri. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.

Before you travel

Accommodation, meals, transport, tickets, permits, inclusions and exclusions are confirmed only in your written quotation. Share accessibility needs, children's ages and your preferred pace when enquiring. Activities depend on weather, local access and availability.
