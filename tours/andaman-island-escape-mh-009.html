Andaman Island Escape

Beaches and island experiences

Places covered

Port Blair → Havelock → Neil Island

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrival in Port Blair. Meet the local representative (if arranged), transfer to the hotel and check in. Depending on arrival time, visit Cellular Jail museum and evening light-and-sound show (tickets permitting). Overnight in Port Blair.

Day 2

Visit Cellular Jail museum and evening light-and-sound show (tickets permitting). Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Port Blair.

Day 3

Visit Corbyn’s Cove beach and local heritage museum. Continue sightseeing in Port Blair at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Port Blair.

Day 4

Travel from Port Blair to Havelock (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Radhanagar Beach and coastal sunset viewpoints. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Havelock.

Day 5

Visit Elephant Beach excursion or snorkelling (sea conditions permitting). Continue sightseeing in Havelock at a relaxed pace, with stops for meals. Overnight in Havelock.

Day 6

Travel from Havelock to Neil Island (journey time to be confirmed). After arrival, Visit Bharatpur Beach and Laxmanpur Beach. Explore nearby streets and local food or craft markets if time permits. Overnight in Neil Island.

Day 7

After breakfast, check out from Neil Island and transfer to the agreed departure point. Plan sufficient time for road conditions, security checks and onward travel.
