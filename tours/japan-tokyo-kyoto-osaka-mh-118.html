Japan Tokyo Kyoto Osaka

Customisable Japan holiday featuring Tokyo, Hakone, Kyoto.

Places covered

Tokyo → Hakone → Kyoto → Osaka

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Tokyo; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Senso-ji Temple and Shibuya crossing. Overnight in Tokyo.

Day 2

Travel from Tokyo to Hakone; visit Lake Ashi and Mount Fuji viewpoints (weather permitting). Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Hakone.

Day 3

Travel from Hakone to Kyoto; visit Fushimi Inari Shrine and Arashiyama. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Kyoto.

Day 4

Travel from Kyoto to Osaka; visit Osaka Castle and Dotonbori. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Osaka.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from Osaka. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
