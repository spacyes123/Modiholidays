Goa Family Beaches & Heritage

Customisable Goa holiday featuring Panaji, North Goa, Old Goa.

Places covered

Panaji → North Goa → Old Goa → South Goa

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Panaji; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Fontainhas and Miramar Beach. Overnight in Panaji.

Day 2

Travel from Panaji to North Goa; visit Fort Aguada and northern beaches. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in North Goa.

Day 3

Travel from North Goa to Old Goa; visit Basilica of Bom Jesus and Se Cathedral. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Old Goa.

Day 4

Travel from Old Goa to South Goa; visit Colva and quieter southern beaches. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in South Goa.

Day 5

After breakfast, check out and depart from South Goa. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
