Spiti Valley Road Expedition

Customisable Himachal Pradesh holiday featuring Shimla, Kalpa, Kaza.

Places covered

Shimla → Kalpa → Kaza → Tabo → Chandratal → Manali

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Shimla; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see The Ridge, Mall Road and Christ Church. Overnight in Shimla.

Day 2

Travel from Shimla to Kalpa; visit Kinnaur Kailash viewpoints. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Kalpa.

Day 3

Travel from Kalpa to Kaza; visit Key Monastery and Kibber village. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Kaza.

Day 4

Travel from Kaza to Tabo; visit Tabo Monastery. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Tabo.

Day 5

Travel from Tabo to Chandratal; visit Chandratal Lake (road access seasonal). Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Chandratal.

Day 6

Travel from Chandratal to Manali; visit Hadimba Temple and Solang Valley. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Manali.

Day 7

After breakfast, check out and depart from Manali. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
