Puri Konark Chilika

Customisable Odisha holiday featuring Bhubaneswar, Puri, Konark.

Places covered

Bhubaneswar → Puri → Konark → Chilika → Bhubaneswar

Day-wise sightseeing itinerary

Suggested itinerary. Exact travel times, hotel availability, entry tickets and activities are confirmed in your quotation.

Day 1

Arrive in Bhubaneswar; transfer and check in after arrival. If time permits, see Lingaraj Temple precinct and Udayagiri caves. Overnight in Bhubaneswar.

Day 2

Travel from Bhubaneswar to Puri; visit Jagannath Temple surroundings and Puri beach. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Puri.

Day 3

Travel from Puri to Konark; visit Konark Sun Temple. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Konark.

Day 4

Travel from Konark to Chilika; visit Chilika Lake viewpoints and optional boat ride. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Chilika.

Day 5

Travel from Chilika to Bhubaneswar; visit Lingaraj Temple precinct and Udayagiri caves. Admission, activity timings and accessibility are subject to local conditions. Overnight in Bhubaneswar.

Day 6

After breakfast, check out and depart from Bhubaneswar. Departure transfer, road timing and any onward flight or train will be confirmed in the final quotation.
