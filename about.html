Creating Travel Memories Since 2009

At Modi Holidays, our journey began in 2009 with a simple vision: to bring people together through memorable travel experiences.

About Modi Holidays

Every journey deserves personal attention.

What started as a passion for organising holidays has grown into a commitment to helping families, friends and groups discover destinations across India and around the world.

We believe a holiday is about spending time with loved ones, discovering new cultures and enjoying the journey. From scenic domestic holidays to international adventures and carefully planned group tours, we aim to make travel comfortable and memorable.

Our Philosophy

Every traveller is different. We listen to your preferences, plan practical itineraries and help you choose suitable holiday experiences.

Our Promise

  • Personalised holiday planning
  • Thoughtfully designed itineraries
  • Domestic and international destinations
  • Family and group travel arrangements
  • Transparent communication
  • Dedicated assistance throughout planning
