Proposed Standard Terms & Conditions

Proposed Version 2.1 · Draft only · No effective date

OWNER & LEGAL REVIEW DRAFT — NOT APPROVED, ACTIVE OR EFFECTIVE. This page records proposed wording only. It is not current customer policy and must not be relied on as an agreement. It will not take effect unless the owner approves the commercial provisions, Indian legal counsel reviews them, and the company separately authorises activation. Proposed Version 2.1 remains inactive.

1. Common company-wide terms

1.1 Enquiries, availability and confirmation

A website request is an enquiry, not a booking. Packages, hotels, rooms, transport, sightseeing and related services are subject to availability. A quotation is a proposal and does not reserve services. Payment of an advance would not by itself guarantee a booking. Under this proposal, a booking would be confirmed only when Modi Holidays issues written confirmation identifying the confirmed services.

1.2 Customer acceptance

Before requesting payment, Modi Holidays would provide the applicable quotation, the identified Standard T&C version, the applicable cancellation schedule and any relevant supplier-specific exceptions. The customer would explicitly accept them through an authorised WhatsApp conversation, email or recorded digital acceptance. The acceptance record would identify the customer, timestamp, quotation version, T&C version and supplier exceptions disclosed.

1.3 Changes to an accepted quotation

A material change to price, dates, accommodation, inclusions, cancellation obligations or supplier conditions would require fresh written customer acceptance before further payment or commitments based on the revised arrangements. Non-material administrative corrections would be documented and communicated.

1.4 Official communication, complaints and disruption

Confirmations, cancellations, payment instructions and material changes would use authorised company channels identified in the applicable travel documents. Customers would be asked to report service concerns promptly. For disruption, Modi Holidays would communicate material changes and available alternatives. Remedies would remain subject to the accepted booking terms and applicable law; nothing in this proposal removes statutory consumer rights.

1.5 Privacy

Customer information would be handled under the applicable privacy notice and data-protection requirements. Information needed to arrange travel may be shared with relevant service providers for the disclosed purposes and as permitted by law.

2. Package-specific conditions

2.1 Inclusions and exclusions

A package price would include only services expressly identified as included in the accepted quotation. Airfare, rail tickets, visas, insurance, meals, attraction fees, personal expenses and other services would be excluded unless expressly listed as included.

2.2 Documents and special requirements

Travellers would provide accurate details and carry required travel documents. Visa assistance would not guarantee visa issuance or admission. Insurance would be included only when stated in the quotation. Accessibility, dietary, medical and other special requirements should be disclosed before confirmation; arrangements would be confirmed only in writing.

2.3 Supplier exceptions

Before acceptance, material supplier conditions would identify the affected service, non-refundable amount or calculation method, relevant deadlines, restrictions and any exception to the proposed standard cancellation schedule. An exception would need to be disclosed and accepted before booking, supported by documented costs and permitted by applicable law.

3. Cancellation and refunds

The proposed cancellation bands, calculation method, supplier-cost treatment, refund calculation and target processing periods are set out in the Proposed Cancellation & Refund Schedule. That linked page is also an inactive owner/legal review draft. No percentage, fee, deadline or refund commitment on either page is currently approved policy.

4. Proposed payment terms

For owner and legal review only, the proposal states a 25% booking advance and the remaining 75% at least 15 calendar days before departure; bookings made within 15 days would require full payment at booking. A supplier-specific exception would need disclosure and acceptance before booking. If a payment were overdue, the proposal provides for written notice with 48 hours to pay, subject to a previously disclosed earlier supplier deadline. No payment method, bank detail, surcharge or payment-processing fee is created here. Any payment would be due only under a separately approved and issued quotation and confirmation.

5. Taxes and additional charges

Applicable taxes, statutory charges and other payable amounts would be identified in the quotation as required by law. Customer-requested services or changes could carry charges agreed in advance. These proposed terms establish no universal tax rate or automatic additional charge.

6. Hotel availability and substitution

Hotels, room categories, locations and meal plans would be identified in the quotation where available. “Proposed,” “tentative” or “or similar” accommodation would not necessarily be a confirmed reservation. If a proposed or confirmed hotel could not be provided, Modi Holidays would seek an equivalent or better alternative using reasonably comparable location, room category, amenities and meal plan. A material substitution would require written customer approval. If no acceptable alternative were available, an appropriate price adjustment or cancellation remedy would be offered consistently with approved booking conditions and applicable law.

7. Document precedence and version protection — proposed

7.1 Mandatory law

Mandatory legal requirements would take precedence over conflicting contractual wording.

7.2 Booking-specific terms

Subject to applicable law, the expressly accepted booking-specific quotation and disclosed exceptions would govern the matters they identify. The accepted Standard T&C version would apply to matters not otherwise specifically agreed.

7.3 Supplier conditions

Supplier terms would apply only to the extent properly disclosed and incorporated into the booking agreement. Any material conflict would need to be resolved before booking confirmation. A supplier-specific condition varying a company-wide term would identify the affected service and exact exception, be disclosed to and accepted by the customer before booking, and be recorded with the accepted quotation. It would apply only to the identified service and accepted exception; it would not silently amend other terms. Supplier charges would require actual documented costs, applicable recoveries credited and duplicate recovery prevented, consistently with applicable law.

7.4 Existing bookings

An existing booking would retain the quotation, Standard T&C version and supplier-specific exceptions disclosed and accepted for that booking. A later T&C version would not apply retroactively. The accepted quotation and T&C snapshot would remain preserved and would not be overwritten. A material variation would be recorded separately, identify the change, be accepted by the customer in writing before implementation and remain linked to the original booking, subject to mandatory law.

Owner and legal decisions still required

The cancellation percentages and no-show charge; payment schedule and default process; refund calculation and timing; evidence and credit treatment for supplier costs; hotel equivalence and remedies; authorised communication channels; privacy and record-retention details; and legal entity, address, governing-law and dispute wording all require owner confirmation and Indian legal review. No historic quotation terms are adopted by this draft.
